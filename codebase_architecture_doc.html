7. Step-by-Step Story of the 4 Key Pipelines

👶 Pipeline 1: How does it know the exact distance between two ports?

Files: src/App.tsx → python_engine/c_cpp_bridge.py → c_cpp_engine/maritime_math.c

  • Step 1: You pick "Newcastle (Australia)" and "Paradip Port (India)".
  • Step 2: Python calls the compiled C math engine (maritime_math.c) like a calculator rocket.
  • Step 3: The C code curves around the Earth's sphere and outputs: "Exactly 5,120 Nautical Miles — will take 17.1 days at 12.5 knots speed!" in less than 2 microseconds.

👶 Pipeline 2: How does KAVACH check the weather?

Files: python_engine/weather_kavach.py → src/components/sagarsetu/KAVACHWeatherCard.tsx

  • Step 1: It splits the trip into 3 checkpoints: (1) Origin Harbor, (2) Middle of the Ocean, (3) Destination Harbor.
  • Step 2: It checks wind speed and wave swell height at all 3 checkpoints.
  • Step 3: It combines them into a single score: Risk = 25% Start + 50% Sea Lane + 25% End. If waves are under 2 meters, it shows a green "Favorable" badge!

👶 Pipeline 3: How does it pick the best ship for maximum savings?

Files: c_cpp_engine/freight_optimizer.cpp ↔ src/App.tsx

  • Step 1: It tests all 10 ship sizes (from tiny 35,000-ton Handysize to giant 208,000-ton Newcastlemax).
  • Step 2: It throws out ships that are too deep to enter the port's shallow water.
  • Step 3: It tests if 1 big ship is cheaper than 2 smaller ships (consolidating 2 trips into 1 saves 40% in port and fuel bills!).
  • Step 4: It highlights the winning vessel: "Suggested for Max Savings: Capesize (Net Savings: +$3.50/MT)".

👶 Pipeline 4: How does it calculate the Carbon Green Score?

Files: python_engine/carbon_cii.py → src/components/sagarsetu/GreenFreightCarbon.tsx

  • Step 1: Takes the 17 days of sailing × 32.4 tons of fuel per day = 550 tons of fuel.
  • Step 2: Multiplies fuel by 3.114 (IMO standard) = 1,712 tons of CO₂ smoke.
  • Step 3: Because 1 giant Capesize carries 160,000 tons of cargo at once, the pollution per ton is tiny! It awards the ship an "A" Grade and a 92/100 Green Score.
